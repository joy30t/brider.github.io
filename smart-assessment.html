Our Process

How We Maximise Every Placement

Three integrated stages ensure the right candidate reaches the right role, with data confirming every decision.

🎯

Stage 1, Candidate Assessment

Every candidate is evaluated across four dimensions: technical job skills, soft skills and personality, language proficiency, and career goals. We use scientifically validated tools that are designed for multilingual, multicultural workforces, reducing bias and improving predictive accuracy.

Psychometric Testing DISC Personality Profile Skills Simulation Language Assessment Cognitive Ability Tests
📊

Stage 2, Market Intelligence Cross-Check

We validate every candidate profile against real-time Caribbean and Suriname labour market data. Compensation packages are benchmarked against current salary surveys. Skill demand is cross-referenced with ILO, World Bank, and IDB regional data, ensuring the placement is sustainable and competitively priced.

ILO Labour Data Salary Benchmarking IDB Skill Demand Reports World Bank Indicators
🤝

Stage 3, Strategic Placement & Follow-Up

Assessment data is matched to your role requirements and organisational culture. After placement, we track quality-of-hire at 30, 60, and 90 days. Outcomes feed back into our models, making each subsequent placement smarter than the last.

Skills-to-Role Matching Culture Fit Analysis 90-Day Performance Tracking Retention Monitoring
